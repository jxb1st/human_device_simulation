TASK REQUIREMENTS AND ENVIRONMENT RULES
task: none given for this session
rules:
1. Feet are fixed: every key, part, place and object listed is within arm's reach; nothing else is.
2. Each hand holds one thing at most; a move ends with the hand empty, so nothing is carried from one command to the next.
3. A press counts only when the key travels 1.2 mm; the device, not the request, decides what the display shows.
4. A knob or dial is re-gripped every 75°; a setting outside the control's range is refused.
5. POWER switches the machine on or off; with it on, FILTER SIZE switches the filter basket between single and double wall, and PROGRAM then 1 CUP or 2 CUP runs the key (2 mL per second) until the same key is pressed again, which stores that volume (20–90 mL).
6. The grinder runs while the portafilter or the dosing cup sits in the grinder cradle with the hopper seated, its lid on and beans in it: it grinds the dose set on the grind amount dial (8–22 g, 18 g at rest) at 2 g per second into the basket or cup.
7. 1 CUP brews 30 mL and 2 CUP 60 mL, only with the portafilter locked in the group head, tamped grounds in it and a cup or glass under the group head; the flow depends on the grind size (1 finest … 16 coarsest): 60 mL in 25–35 s is in range, faster is too fast (grind finer), slower is choked (grind coarser); the first 5 s are pre-infusion. Pressing the same key again stops the shot.
8. The steam dial has three positions: hot water, off, steam. Steam foams the milk in the jug under the wand from 4 s on (velvety at 8 s, thick foam at 14 s; at least 50 mL); with nothing under the wand it purges into the drip tray. Hot water runs from the hot-water outlet into whatever stands under it at 15 mL per second.
9. The scale shows the weight on it in grams; TARE zeroes it. Setting the portafilter on the knock box knocks the spent puck out.
10. Pouring moves what fits: the amount is capped by what the source holds and by the room in the destination; beans go into the hopper only with its lid off; tamping needs grounds in the basket.
11. A command that cannot be grounded, or whose precondition fails, is refused with a reason and changes nothing.
