Methods

  1. Assets: a CC BY 4.0 microwave MJCF model and a CC0 MakeHuman/MPFB character with an articulated finger rig; a procedural generic printer.
  2. Motion: action primitives (reach, pregrasp, press, pull, push, rotate, pick-and-place) compiled to 100 Hz hand/finger/gaze targets; IK solves the arms; feet are locked; a torso lean is authored when a target is out of straight reach.
  3. Selected physics: the receiving device part (key, tray) is simulated in MuJoCo against a kinematic fingertip proxy; a negative control repeats the test with the hand moved clear.
  4. Recorded trajectory: one run per sequence (trajectory.npz, events.jsonl, manifest.json); all cameras are rendered from that record.
  5. Rendering: Blender Cycles, constant 30 fps, numbered frames encoded to H.264.
